# The dilemma and the insight

Wheat again, fallow, or something new? A wrong guess costs a whole year. Sétif (Algeria) and the US Great Plains face the same choice.
Every field has a memory. NASA has recorded it for 25 years. Nobody lets farmers replay it.

---

# The product

Field Memory, Season Replay, Evidence Cards, 2050, a generative assistant. One screenshot each (capture from guided demo mode).

---

# NASA data is the engine

POWER, IMERG, SMAP, MODIS, HLS, NASADEM, NISAR, GRACE, ECOSTRESS, GEDI, NEX-GDDP-CMIP6, Prithvi: each mapped to the decision it feeds.

---

# Science and honesty

Proof field: history vs USDA crop map (78% of seasons agree) and ranking vs the Akron trial (disagrees), both shown as measured. FAO-56 water balance, uncertainty, five mandatory fields on every card.

---

# Impact

Farmers and extension services; same pipeline globally; validated where records exist; a printable card for the human handoff. Supports, never replaces, local expertise.
